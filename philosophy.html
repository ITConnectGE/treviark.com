Treviark is a house of rarity — a curated realm where living unfolds as a respectful ritual, rooted deeply in a culture of manifold landscapes and botanical integration.
Within the house of Treviark, tea and honey are presented in their most authentic form.
Our teas originate from Georgian plantations left untouched since the early twentieth century, where cold winters naturally protect the bushes and allow them to thrive without artificial influence.
Treviark Blossom Honey is harvested in small batches from Georgian highland apiaries, gathered from seasonal blossoms and preserved in its purest form.
